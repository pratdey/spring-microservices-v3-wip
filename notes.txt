In your application:

1. Who launches the Spring Context?

`SpringApplication.run()` in `SpringBootTest1Application.main()`:

```java
SpringApplication.run(SpringBootTest1Application.class, args);
```

2. Who triggers component scanning?

`@SpringBootApplication` triggers component scanning.

It scans the package:

```text
com.example.springboottest1
```

and its subpackages, finding controllers and components.

3. Who enables auto-configuration?

`@SpringBootApplication` also enables auto-configuration.

It is a combination of:

```java
@Configuration
@EnableAutoConfiguration
@ComponentScan
```

So, `@SpringBootApplication` handles both component scanning and auto-configuration.
